🎈 Squish Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Summary

Squish Pop does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network requests beyond loading the app itself.

What data is stored, and where

Your score, collectible progress, and settings (sound on/off, difficulty) are saved using your browser or device's local storage, directly on your own device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Can be erased at any time by clearing the app's storage/data or uninstalling the app

Third-party services

Squish Pop uses none. No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no crash reporting services, and no third-party libraries that transmit data off the device.

Children's privacy

Squish Pop was built as a personal, ad-free game for a child to play, with no data collection of any kind, no in-app purchases, and no external communication features.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project's GitHub issues page.
